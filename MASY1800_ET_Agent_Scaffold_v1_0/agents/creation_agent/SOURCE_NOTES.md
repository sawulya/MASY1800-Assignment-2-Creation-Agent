# Assignment 2 - Source Notes

Sources used as evidence in the final test outputs:

1. Yao, S. et al. (2022). **ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models.** arXiv:2210.03629. Published October 6, 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629
   - Original research showing interleaved reasoning and task-specific actions.

2. Schick, T. et al. (2023). **Toolformer: Language Models Can Teach Themselves to Use Tools.** arXiv:2302.04761. Published February 9, 2023. https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04761
   - Original research showing model-directed use of external APIs/tools.

3. Anthropic (2024). **Building effective agents.** Published December 19, 2024. https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents
   - First-party engineering guidance on workflows vs. agents and simple composable patterns.

4. OpenAI (2025). **New tools for building agents.** Published March 11, 2025. https://openai.com/index/new-tools-for-building-agents/
   - First-party evidence on Responses API, built-in tools, Agents SDK, and tracing as agent-building infrastructure.

5. OpenAI (2025). **A practical guide to building agents.** https://openai.com/business/guides-and-resources/a-practical-guide-to-building-ai-agents/
   - First-party guidance describing agents as model + tools + instructions and recommending incremental orchestration.

6. FINRA (2024). **Regulatory Notice 24-09: Regulatory Obligations When Using Generative AI and Large Language Models.** Published June 27, 2024. https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/notices/24-09
   - Confirms technology-neutral FINRA rules and securities-law obligations continue to apply to GenAI use.

7. U.S. SEC (2024). **SEC Adopts Rule Amendments to Regulation S-P to Enhance Protection of Customer Information.** Published May 16, 2024. https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2024-58
   - Supports customer-information safeguarding and incident-response implications for broker-dealers.
